FRONT — what it is
TLA-185 is free, open digital infrastructure for landscape architecture: field
registry, schemas, and citation language for studios, universities, and councils.
Cite it. Teach it. Write it into briefs. Do not treat it as a membership
institute, product lock-in, or investment product.

Draft public commitments: human stewardship; global citable structure; renewal
paths for institutional budgets; landscape records as professional assets.
